Founding Coaches

Harleen

Founding Coach

Most people leave a conversation with Harleen feeling two things they did not expect to feel together: heard, and clear.

She has a rare quality in any room she enters. She does not rush toward the answer. She holds the space long enough for you to find it yourself. That quality was not learned in a classroom. It was built across a decade of standing in front of thirty students every day, reading rooms, managing invisible pressures, and learning that the most important thing a person in front of others can do is make them feel that what they are carrying matters.

Her formal training in counselling psychology, positive psychology, and NLP gives her the framework. Her years in education gave her the instinct. The combination is why a conversation with Harleen rarely feels like a session. It feels like a weight you have been carrying finally has somewhere to go.

Karan

Founding Coach

Karan did not learn about resilience in a seminar. He learned it the long way, across nine years of showing up inside one of the world's most demanding corporate environments, managing people through pressure, delivering results through difficult seasons, and quietly figuring out how to keep going without burning out.

What those years taught him was not a productivity system. It was something more useful: that the people who sustain high performance over time are not the ones who push hardest. They are the ones who have learned to manage their inner state as deliberately as they manage their workload. He found that out the hard way, and it cost him years he would rather have had back.

That is why he is here. Not to tell you what to do. To help you get there faster than he did.
